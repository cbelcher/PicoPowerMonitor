Asked Copilot
How can I change the text color?

Response:
Set the Foreground property on the TextBlock (it takes a Brush). Examples:
•	XAML (named color): <TextBlock x:Name="VoltageText" Text="V: -- V" FontSize="32" FontWeight="Bold" FontFamily="Consolas" Foreground="Blue"/>
•	XAML (hex): Foreground="#FFFF0000"  // opaque red
•	XAML (resource): Foreground="{StaticResource MyAccentBrush}"
•	Code-behind (C#): VoltageText.Foreground = new SolidColorBrush(Colors.Red);
Pick the approach that fits your styling strategy (inline, resource, or dynamic).
